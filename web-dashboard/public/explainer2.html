How does it work?

Two pieces working together

1. Silent & Loud Personal Alarm  ·  2. Smartphone App with Off-Grid Mesh Hardware

Each works on its own—and together they turn trusted neighbors into a fast response net.

Personal Alarm — In Disguise

Disguised as everyday items—not a lone siren.

Styled as jewelry, keychains, bags, dolls, or toys so your team can locate your live beacon quickly in an emergency.

Even through walls or behind closed doors, teammates' phones sound an alert and show your live map position when you:

  1. Press the alarm button,
  2. Pull the physical pin, or
  3. Say the voice trigger word — faster than dialing 911.
The App

Your phone becomes a live team map

Plug in the mesh dongle to see your team in real time, send messages over off-grid LoRa radio without cell towers or internet, and get background alerts.

The Network

Sensors extend awareness past line of sight

Detect motion, heat, and movement behind walls or across perimeter nodes—and expand coverage over time.

The App watches your back too

Spots unusual routes, trailing threats, or signal drops—and remembers your last known location in dead zones.

  • Prompts for confirmation when risk is detected, and can alert your team if you don't respond.
  • Stores and retries alerts when moving through basements, tunnels, or low-signal spots.

Voice triggers like "Help" or "Call The Raptors!" alert your team hands-free.

Privacy: You decide who sees you

Always visible

Hidden near home

Ghost mode

Minor accounts

Emergency Override:

When an SOS or serious threat triggers, visibility opens to your team so responders can reach you fast.

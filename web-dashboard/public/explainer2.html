What if you cannot press the alarm?

The App is watching your back too

The Raptor's Call quietly notices when something's off — an unexpected route, stalkers trailing you, or sudden impact.

  • If it detects a real risk, it asks for confirmation.
  • If it can't confirm you're okay, it can sound the alarm on your behalf.
  • If you lose signal in a basement, tunnel, or elevator, the network remembers your last known location and keeps retrying until team members receive the message.

The App can hear you say, "Help", or "Call The Raptors!" and sound the alarm to your team.

Privacy: You decide who sees you

Privacy Options in the Settings Page

Always visible

Your team sees your real location at all times. Built for members actively on watch.

Hidden near home

Your icon disappears automatically within a radius you set around your home, and reappears everywhere else.

Ghost mode

Always hidden from the map during calm moments. Built for neighbors who carry higher personal risk.

Minor accounts

Anyone under 18 is automatically locked to ghost mode — no exceptions, no opt-out.

Emergency Override:

The instant it matters, everyone becomes visible — whether you pull your own alarm, a teammate raises it for you because they see you in danger, or the network itself flags a serious threat. Hidden is a setting for calm moments, never for emergencies.

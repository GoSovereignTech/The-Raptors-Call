What Is the Initiative?

The Sovereign Horizons Initiative is a diaspora-led, land-based development program designed to establish self-sufficient, sovereign communities on the African continent, beginning with a pilot in the Democratic Republic of the Congo.

Our model is built on a Community Land Trust (CLT) structure — land is held in perpetuity for community benefit rather than speculative profit. Residents own the improvements on the land, not the land itself, which keeps costs stable and prevents displacement.

The initiative is organized under the framework of the African Union's recognition of the Global African Diaspora as the 6th Region of Africa. We are not a charity. We are a compact — a binding agreement among people who choose to build together.

Why We Are Asking Black/African Diaspora to Sign

This initiative is not for everyone. It is specifically for people of African descent living outside the continent who understand that the 6th Region is not a metaphor.

  • The diaspora holds the capital, technical expertise, and strategic distance needed to build without being immediately co-opted by local patronage networks or foreign corporate capture.
  • Africa's development has been systematically sabotaged by external actors who profit from instability. A diaspora-led model is harder to capture because its members are not dependent on local political favors for survival.
  • The 6th Region framework gives us political leverage. When we approach African governments as a coordinated diaspora bloc, we negotiate from a position of collective strength.
  • We are not asking for charity. We are asking for committed participants who will contribute skills, resources, and presence.

What Kind of People We Want

Character

  • People of good character who do not believe in following where colonizers left off to continue the colonial exploitation of the people.
  • People who deliberately reject the systems and language of colonization. We no longer use "His/Her Excellency." When the United States broke free from England, it did not retain such language. We address each other as equals.
  • People who respect indigenous land rights and will not continue the dispossession of natives nor relegate them to reservations.
  • People who are emotionally sound and can resist the control of negative impulses over character and behavior. We need people who can disagree without fragmenting.

Geopolitical Sophistication

  • People who are well-read and will not fall for state capture, corporate capture, colonization schemes, or divide-and-conquer tactics.
  • People who recognize bribes for what they are — paper printed money offered in exchange for African sovereignty and resources.
  • People who can imagine a world without the dogma of neocolonization. This project requires imagination, not just reaction.

Skills We Actively Seek

Engineers · Medical professionals · Educators · Legal professionals · Security specialists · Agronomists · ISSB construction experts · Finance professionals · Technologists · Community organizers

Our Unique and Advanced Initial Offerings

🛡️ Defense Technology Transfer

Proprietary defense inventions designed to protect people and farms from militia threats. Deployed in partnership with local authorities and community safety committees — not as private militias, which are illegal under DRC law.

We do not use the word "police." The term carries the weight of colonial enforcement and slavery. We use "Community Safety" and "Peacekeeping" instead.

🌾 Food Sovereignty & Seed Technology

  • Unprecedented variety of climate-adapted seed stocks
  • Community-scale agricultural systems designed for abundance, not scarcity
  • Seed bank infrastructure to preserve and distribute sovereign seed

⛽ Alternative Fuel Technology

  • Ethanol production systems to replace dependence on imported gasoline
  • Community-scale fuel micro-economies

💧 Water Sovereignty

  • Atmospheric Water Generation (AWG) systems with solar integration
  • Rainwater harvesting and community water management

🏗️ Home Building Sovereignty

Interlocking Stabilized Soil Blocks (ISSB) for inexpensive and rapid modern home construction. UN-Habitat documents ISSB as approximately 30% cheaper and faster than fired bricks, with lower environmental impact.

📚 Sovereign Education System

  • Time-based currency to power education, ensuring every member has access to learning
  • Curriculum teaching sovereignty principles, practical skills, and geopolitical awareness

🚐 Mobile Home Accommodation

Visitors and prospective members are encouraged to bring or use mobile homes during the evaluation phase.

Note: This document must comply with DRC Law No. 004/2001 and requires notarization. The founding members charged with administration must provide certificates of good conduct, life, and morals.

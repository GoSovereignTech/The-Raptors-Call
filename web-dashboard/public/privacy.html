Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

What we collect

When you grant precise location, your GPS coordinates are shared with vetted members of your community on a peer-to-peer mesh network. We do not store these coordinates on any central server.

Who can see your location

Only members who have completed in-person vetting and a background check. Known abusers are refused access. All visibility is reciprocal — if you can see someone, they can see you.

Your identity

Your map presence is an anonymous ID, not your name. Nobody knows who is who except by your own choice to reveal.

Where your data lives

All operational data stays on your device (local SQLite) and inside the mesh network. No central server, no analytics, no advertising profiles.

Third parties

We do not sell, share, or transmit your data to any third party. There are no tracking pixels, no ad networks, no analytics services.

Age requirement

You must be 13 years or older to use this service. Users under 18 require parental consent.

Your rights

You can clear your location data at any time by clearing your browser storage. There is no account to delete.

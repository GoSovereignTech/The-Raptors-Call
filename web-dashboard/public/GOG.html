About the Founder

Stephen Sutherland, MD — Howard University College of Medicine, Class of 2014.

Before medical school, Dr. Sutherland worked as a high school and elementary school teacher, a web developer, and a music school founder. After graduating in 2014, during the nationwide Black Lives Matter protests, he discovered severe epidemic statistics in Black communities that were never taught.

He had a choice: work in hospitals, earn good money, live comfortably, and leave the big problems to someone else; or dedicate his entire career to solving the socioeconomic crises destroying Black communities globally. He chose the latter.

He found that most of these epidemics are relatively new. In the 1950s, Black Americans were not doing epidemic gang violence anywhere in the USA. Black communities were filled with the sound of happy children, not the sound of guns. The trend lines are getting worse in every category.

He decided that solving these problems is the most important thing to do with his career. To start, he researched and wrote the book Guardians of Greatness: The Greatest Renaissance of the New Millennium. He has since run social experiments and projects in Baltimore, Haiti, and Nigeria.

“I refuse to watch another generation of Black children grow up in trauma. I refuse to accept that imperialism and exploitation are inevitable. Someone has to build the future. Someone has to create the solutions. That someone is me.”

"I'm not here to get rich. I'm here to stop the dying."

Data Deletion Policy

Last updated: 2026 · Companion to the Privacy Policy.

1. Summary

You have two ways to remove your data: self-service for anything stored on your own device, and a formal request for anything stored on our server (vetting records).

  • Location, chat, saved map tiles, and settings live on your device. You can clear them yourself in a few seconds — see Section 3.
  • Vetting records (interview notes, collateral references, background-check status, incident log) live on our server and require a formal request — see Section 4.
  • Some records must be retained for a period required by law (FCRA adverse-action records, active incident investigations). Those exceptions are listed in Section 6.
  • We complete formal deletion requests within 30 days, and notify you when done — see Section 7.

2. Two kinds of data

Data deletion depends on where the data lives.

2.1 On your device (self-service)

Your browser's local storage and IndexedDB hold your cached map tiles, saved location, chat log, and settings. This data never left your device, so you control it entirely.

2.2 On our server (formal request)

Your vetting record — identity details, interview notes, collateral reference answers, background-check status, reviewer decision, and incident log — lives on our secured server. Only authenticated reviewers can access it. Deleting it requires a formal request so we can verify your identity, apply retention exceptions, and audit the deletion.

3. Self-service deletion (on your device)

You can clear device-stored data immediately. No request needed, no waiting period.

3.1 Clear location and settings

Open the app and go to Settings → Clear local data. This removes your saved location, nickname, mesh node ID, chat log, and preferences from this device.

3.2 Clear cached map tiles and app data

If you want to wipe everything the app has stored in your browser, clear site data for the app's domain in your browser settings:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Third-party cookies → See all site data and permissions → find the app's domain → Delete.
  • Safari: Settings → Safari → Advanced → Website Data → find the domain → Delete.
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data → Manage Data → find the domain → Remove.
What this does: Removes all device-stored data. Your server-side vetting record is not affected — you will need a formal request for that.

4. Formal deletion request (on our server)

To delete your vetting record, submit a formal request. The process has three steps:

How to submit

1
Email us from the address on file (or provide a phone number we can verify against your vetting record) with the subject line Data Deletion Request.
2
Include your full legal name as it appears in your vetting record, and your mesh node ID (if you know it). Do not include your Social Security Number or a copy of your ID unless we specifically ask — we will only ask if we cannot verify you otherwise.
3
We confirm receipt within 5 business days, verify your identity, apply the retention exceptions in Section 6, and complete deletion within 30 days of verification.

What you cannot request. You cannot request deletion of an incident report filed about you while it is under active investigation, or of FCRA adverse-action records during the required retention period. You can request deletion after the investigation closes or the retention period ends. See Section 6.

5. What we delete, anonymize, or retain

The table below shows what happens to each category of server-side data when a formal deletion request is completed.

Data category Action Notes
Identity details (name, address, phone, emergency contact) Deleted Removed from the vetting table. No copy retained after deletion.
Interview notes and behavioural answers Deleted Removed from the vetting table.
Collateral reference names, phones, and answers Deleted References' personal data removed alongside yours.
Compatibility profile (Big Five, attachment, hobbies, goals) Deleted Stored separately and deleted in the same request.
Reviewer decision and reason category Anonymized Kept as an aggregate, non-identifying record to demonstrate that the vetting process was followed. Your name and ID are stripped; only the decision and date remain.
Background-check status (pass/flag) Deleted from our server The screening agency holds its own copy. Contact the agency separately to delete their copy.
FCRA adverse-action notices Retained up to 5 years Required to demonstrate FCRA compliance. Contains only the date, category, and summary — not the underlying consumer report.
Incident reports (filed about you or by you) Anonymized after investigation Incident content is anonymized after the investigation closes, but the date, severity, and outcome are retained for community safety history.
Active incident investigations Retained until closed Cannot be deleted while an investigation is active. Deleted or anonymized once closed.

6. Retention exceptions

We cannot delete the following, even on request, until the stated condition is met:

  1. Active incident investigations. Deleting evidence during an investigation could endanger community members. We delete or anonymize once the investigation closes.
  2. FCRA adverse-action records. Retained for the period required to demonstrate compliance (typically up to 5 years).
  3. Legal holds. If we are required by law, subpoena, or court order to preserve data, we will retain it until the legal obligation ends and notify you if legally permitted.
  4. Aggregate anonymized statistics. Once data is truly anonymized (no re-identification possible), it is no longer personal data and is not subject to deletion. This is limited to counts and outcomes, never individual records.

7. Timeline

  • Within 5 business days: we confirm receipt of your request and, if needed, ask for identity verification.
  • Within 10 business days: we complete identity verification.
  • Within 30 days of verification: we complete deletion and send you a written confirmation listing what was deleted, what was anonymized, and what was retained under Section 6.
  • If we cannot complete in 30 days: we notify you before the deadline with the reason and a new date (no more than 60 additional days).

8. Backups

Server-side data is backed up nightly. When we delete your record, the live database is updated immediately, and the backup containing your data is purged within the next backup rotation cycle — typically 30 days.

Backups are encrypted at rest and accessible only to the system administrator. They are never used to restore individual records except in the event of a full system recovery, in which case any deleted records are re-deleted as part of the recovery process.

9. Verification

We verify your identity before deleting server-side data to prevent someone else from deleting your record. Verification may include:

  • Matching the email or phone number on file to your vetting record.
  • Confirming your full legal name and mesh node ID.
  • If neither is possible, a short video call with a reviewer.

We will never ask for your Social Security Number, a copy of your driver's license, or your password by email. If you receive a request like that, it is a phishing attempt — report it to us.

10. Complaints

If you believe we have not handled your deletion request correctly, you may:

  • Reply to our confirmation email and ask for a review by a second administrator.
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority (EU/UK) or your state attorney general (US).
  • Contact us directly at the address in Section 11.

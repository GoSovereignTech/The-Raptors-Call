Why now

The statistics are not anecdotes. They are patterns — and many are getting worse. We are documenting them, confronting them, and building systems to change them.

11,000+

Reported anti-Black hate crimes

Reported anti-Black hate crimes have surpassed 11,000 annually, with estimates suggesting twice as many go unreported.

Highest in a decade

Suspected lynchings

Black people found hanging from trees — suspected lynchings — are at their highest levels in a decade, according to community monitors and advocates.

600,000

Reported missing each year

About 600,000 people are reported missing each year in the U.S. Only about 2% are abducted off the street or from their cars — but those cases are rising.

2%

Black discretionary spending

Only about 2% of Black discretionary spending is spent with Black-owned businesses. We are building pathways to redirect that circulation.

95% → 65%

Family structure shift

In the 1950s, 95% of Black American families were two-parent homes. Today, about 65% of Black American families are single-parent homes.

New epidemics

Not our historical norms

Epidemic gang violence, mass incarceration, childhood suicide attempts, and family breakdown are relatively new — and therefore solvable.

Recent case: On October 6, 2026, Pikesville, Maryland resident Yvonna Stevens reported narrowly escaping an attempted kidnapping at Quarry Lake Greenspring in Pikesville, Baltimore County. According to her account, three white men in a white van tried to pull her inside. She described one of the men as wearing a kippah. This story has received little mainstream coverage.

Founder

Stephen Sutherland, MD — Howard University College of Medicine, Class of 2014.

Before medical school, Dr. Sutherland worked as a high school and elementary school teacher, a web developer, and a music school founder. After graduating in 2014, during the nationwide Black Lives Matter protests, he discovered severe epidemic statistics in Black communities that was never taught.

He found that most of these epidemics are relatively new. For example, in the 1950s, Black Americans were not doing epidemic gang violence anywhere in the USA. Black communities were filled with the sound of happy children, not the sound of guns. The trend lines are getting worse in every category.

He decided that solving these problems is the most important thing to do with his career. To start, he researched and wrote the book the Guardians of Greatness - presents The Greatest Renaissance of the New Millennium It presents an truly awakening experience and blueprint to end our epidemic statistics. He has since run social experiments and very noble projects in Baltimore, Haiti, and Nigeria.

“Some would say I failed. But I call them 1st and 2nd iterations. From those iterations, I learned all the unspoken reasons why our communities struggle. My current solutions builds on the wisdom of the past 10+ years. I'm proud of the effort that led to the current amazing batch of solutions. World changing success are just a step away.

The Young Guardians & Guardians of Greatness after-school program was designed to create structure, opportunities, and incentives so our youth are raised with our best efforts — instead of being raised by the streets.

Core conclusion

Black leaders and Black initiatives have failed by the numbers. All the numbers are the worst and peaking. Why? They had no specific goals for reducing these numbers. We are changing that with public, measurable targets.

Initiatives

Guardians of Greatness builds practical, open, and scalable systems. Each initiative attacks a specific statistic.

Raptor’s Call Network

A low-cost community safety network for villages, farms, and neighborhoods: pull-pin alarms, GPS beacons, LoRa/Meshtastic mesh, solar relay nodes, radar/PIR/thermal sensors, live dashboard, and “Rewind” path history.

Read the explainer →

Live Free App

A therapist-driven mental health app for remote therapeutic monitoring, AI-assisted dialogue, vision boards, crisis keyword detection, and billing under CPT 90832, 90834, 90837, and 98978.

Young Guardians

After-school structure, aviation and real-estate design exposure, mentorship, and incentives designed to redirect youth from gang violence and into productive pathways.

Economic Sovereignty

Redirecting a portion of the $300 billion in Black discretionary spending back into Black-owned businesses through a publicly owned social media platform. Past founders will be invited to join as executives; users will own shares.

Food & Fuel Sovereignty

Automated duckweed bioreactors, biofloc fish and shrimp farming, agroforestry, and community-managed ethanol and biogas pathways for African villages and underserved communities.

Family & Relationships

Millennium Relationships and the GOGPersonality app — tools to improve family unity, relationship stability, and the two-parent household rate.

1-year targets

We are promising measurable progress within one year. We will publish updates every two months.

  • Safety: Drop hate crimes and lynchings by more than 50% via the Raptor’s Call network. Drop violent crimes and make African villages safer. Restart farming — measured by counts.
  • Suicide reduction: Deploy the Live Free App to therapists and clients to intervene earlier and reduce suicide attempts.
  • Business creation & revenue: Redirect some of our $300 billion in discretionary spending back to our community through a publicly owned social media platform.
  • Family: Improve family unity and relationships via Millennium Relationships and related tools.
